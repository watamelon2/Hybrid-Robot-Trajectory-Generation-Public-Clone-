## D. Theory — paper-grade open problems

From `docs/theory/07_minimax_regret_synthesis.md` §7 (the
closing document of the seven-doc bundle), seven open
problems are tracked:

1. **LSI verification on Robomimic** (~3-5 days).  Empirically
   verify the log-Sobolev assumption on the demo distribution
   for `can/ph` / `square/ph` / `lift/ph`; check whether the
   $\alpha(\lambda)$ constant matches measured contraction.
2. **Mode-separation tightening on Square** (~1 week).
   Square's 8.3 % limit is currently attributed to retrieval-
   representation; tighten this to a formal mode-separation
   lower bound under the current cost.
3. **Finite-$N$ theory with explicit constants** (~2 weeks).
   The bundle's bounds are asymptotic in $M$ and $N$; finite-
   sample constants for the practical regime ($M\!=\!16$,
   $N\!=\!20$) would close the gap to deployment.
4. **Beyond log-concavity via stochastic localisation**
   (~3 weeks).  Drop the log-concavity assumption from doc 06
   using stochastic localisation; needed for multimodal demo
   distributions that the LSI doesn't cover.
5. **Connection to RLHF / preference learning** (~3-4 weeks).
   The KL-regularised soft-control formulation (doc 01) is
   structurally identical to RLHF's KL-to-reference penalty;
   formalise the connection.
6. **Continuous-time limit at infinite $K$** (~3-5 weeks).
   Take the limit $K \to \infty$ in doc 06's predictor-
   corrector SDE; should recover the score-bridge as a
   continuous-time soft-control optimum.
7. **The "very good prior" second-order theory** (~2 weeks).
   When the prior is already near the cost optimum (R1
   small-lift regime from Slate O), the leading-order SNIS
   bound is loose; derive a second-order theory specific to
   this regime.

Items 1-3 are the **immediate research follow-up** (a single
research cycle); items 4-7 are paper- or research-program-
grade.
