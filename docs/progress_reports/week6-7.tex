\section{Summary of Initial Idea and Results}

We combine an offline generative trajectory prior with the
analytical score of model-based diffusion (MBD)~\cite{mbd}
via the composite score $\nabla\log p^{\rm data}_t
+\lambda(\sigma_t)\nabla\log p^{\rm model}_t$, where
$p^{\rm model}\!\propto\!\exp(-J(\tau)/\beta)$ and $J$ is a
hand-designed cost. \textbf{Central question}: can a learned
demonstration prior cross the binary-success threshold on
robomimic manipulation, where neither MBD nor behaviour
cloning (BC) succeeds alone? Week-4 baselines on
\texttt{PickPlaceCan} ($N\!=\!60$): demo replay $\mathbf{0.90}$,
BC $\mathbf{0.067}$, MBD alone $\mathbf{0.00}$, and a
\emph{warm-start ablation} $\mathbf{0.017}$ in which BC sets
only the first denoising sample then plays no further role.
Behavioural readouts (no-reach $53\%\!\to\!12\%$; hybrid beat
BC on $85\%$ of paired seeds) showed the prior was influencing
search; Week 6--7 asks whether per-step prior injection crosses
the threshold.

\section{Updated Method}

We evaluate one update rule: each of $M$ perturbed trajectories
$\tau_t^{(m)}$ receives a Gaussian pull
$-\lambda(\sigma_t)(\tau_t^{(m)}-\tau^{\rm prior})/\sigma_t^2$
toward the prior at \emph{every} MBD iteration $t$, \emph{before}
the usual Boltzmann reweighting against $J$ ($\lambda\!=\!0$
recovers MBD; $\lambda\!\to\!\infty$ recovers prior-only
denoising). Four prior families share this rule: (a)~BC; (b)~flow-matching
$v_\theta$~\cite{rectflow} (raw v1, $\sigma$-perturbed v2);
(c)~zero-train 1-NN retrieval against the demonstration bank,
with soft-NN $w_i\!\propto\!\exp(-\beta d_i^2)$ (large $\beta$
selects a single demonstration \emph{mode}); (d)~trained
diffusion policy $v_\theta$~\cite{dp}, a UNet trained 350
epochs on action-chunk denoising conditioned on 2-step obs
history (7 checkpoints, weakly- to over-trained).
\textbf{Infrastructure fix}: the standard chunk-boundary
\texttt{env.reset()+set\_sim\_state()} wipes robosuite's OSC
state, dropping the $\lambda\!=\!0$ MBD-only sanity gate to
$0/50$; gating the reset on $K\!>\!0$ restores it to $8/10$.

\section{Updated Example}

Two tracks at the canonical operating point $K\!=\!10$,
$M\!=\!16$, $\sigma_{\max}\!=\!0.25$, $N\!=\!60$\,rollouts/cell
(see appendix for symbols). \textbf{Track A} (cross-task):
retrieval prior on four robomimic-\texttt{ph} tasks at
$\lambda\!=\!1$. \textbf{Track B} (prior-quality $\times$
MBD-budget grid, trained DP on \texttt{can}): 7 checkpoints
$\times K\!\in\!\{5,10,20\}$ at $\lambda\!=\!0.5$ plus a
$\lambda$-sweep at the weakest. A cell is a \emph{lift} iff
its Wilson CI lower bound exceeds the same-checkpoint
prior-only CI upper bound by $\geq 6$ pp (pre-registered).

Track A: retrieval gives $\mathbf{0.738}$ on \texttt{can}
(4-seed pool $n\!=\!240$), $\mathbf{1.000}$ on \texttt{lift},
$\mathbf{0.083}$ on \texttt{square} (raw observations cannot
disambiguate contact-similar phases), $0/40$ on
\texttt{tool\_hang} (now-patched sparse-reward bug). Track B:
the hybrid matches every standalone DP checkpoint at $K\!=\!10$;
the only cell clearing the pre-registered rule is moderate-prior
$+$ larger budget (epoch 149, $K\!=\!20$, $\mathbf{0.833}$,
$+10$ pp lift). \textbf{Adaptation (bank-size scaling)}: sweeping
$N_b\!\in\!\{5,10,20,50,100,200\}$ on \texttt{can} gives
$20\%\!\to\!35\%$ at $N_b\!\le\!10$, then a flat $\mathbf{68.3\%}$
plateau across $N_b\!\in\![20,100]$: the prior is
coverage-limited, not size-limited, ruling out a
``more data'' explanation for \texttt{square}'s ceiling.
\textbf{Mechanism}: MBD lifts the prior iff (a)~its score is
informative at the operating noise level and (b)~it has not
saturated the task; flow fails (a), BC clears (a) only with the
bridge, retrieval and moderate-DP clear both.

\section*{Appendix: Hyperparameter Glossary}

$K$ = MBD denoising iterations per planning step.
$M$ = perturbed trajectories sampled per iteration (Monte-Carlo
budget for the Boltzmann reweighting).
$\sigma_t$ = noise std at iteration $t$, following a cosine
schedule from $\sigma_{\max}\!=\!0.25$ to $\sigma_{\min}\!=\!0.01$.
$\lambda(\sigma_t)$ = weight on the prior-pull term in the
composite-score update; $\lambda\!=\!0$ recovers MBD, large
$\lambda$ recovers prior-only denoising.
$\beta$ = inverse-temperature in soft-NN retrieval
($w_i\!\propto\!\exp(-\beta d_i^2)$); large $\beta$ selects a
single demonstration mode, small $\beta$ averages across
demonstrations.
$N$ = number of evaluation rollouts per cell ($N\!=\!60$ unless
noted; 4-seed pools quote $n\!=\!240$).
$R$ = MPC replans per rollout ($R\!=\!1$).
$N_b$ = retrieval bank size (demos available to the 1-NN
lookup); the canonical operating point uses $N_b\!=\!200$.
